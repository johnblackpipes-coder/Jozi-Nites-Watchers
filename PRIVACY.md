# Privacy & POPIA — Jozi Nites Watchers

This repository contains the public front-end for Jozi Nites Watchers. Below is a short POPIA- and privacy-friendly summary you can publish for event participants.

## What we collect
- Display name (required in signup form)
- Optional contact details (email or social handle) if you choose to provide them
- Event requests (movie title + preferred date) submitted by participants

We collect only the information required to coordinate watch events.

## Why we collect it
- To manage event invites and communication
- To coordinate scheduling and movie selection

## How we store and use it
- Form responses (Google Forms) are stored in Google Forms / Google Sheets controlled by the organisers.
- Quick in-page requests (the mini-form) are stored locally in the user's browser unless you configure a server or Apps Script endpoint.
- We do not share personal data outside the organisers without your explicit consent.

## Retention
- By default we retain event-related details for 12 months, or until you request deletion. Change this retention period to suit your needs.
