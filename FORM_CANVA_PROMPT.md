Canva design brief / prompt — Google Form template for Jozi Nites Watchers

Use this brief to design a Google Forms visual in Canva (create images/backgrounds/buttons for the form header and thumbnail assets). Include downloadable assets for the form header (PNG/SVG) and three preview thumbnails.

Design goals
- Dark, cinematic, slightly playful; evocative of Johannesburg nightlife but tasteful and respectful.
- Primary palette: deep charcoal (#0a0a0a), accent red (#e50914), gold accent (#d4af37), soft gray text (#a3a3a3).
- Typography: Heading font with vintage typewriter vibe (Special Elite), body text Inter or a neutral sans.
- Mood: warm, inclusive, slightly naughty but consensual tone.

Assets to create (sizes & file types)
1) Form header image (for Google Form banner)
   - Size: 1600 x 400 px (landscape). Provide PNG (transparent or dark background) and JPG versions.
   - Visual: subtle grainy film texture background, faint city skyline silhouette, director's chair motif (use the provided logo), small pineapple motif near corner (tasteful).
   - Overlay: form title text "Jozi Nites Watchers — Sign up" in Special Elite; subtitle in Inter: "Lekker movies. Naughty nights. Perfectly sync." Use gold accent for key words.

2) Three preview thumbnail images (for coming-soon previews)
   - Size: 800 x 450 px (16:9). Produce three distinct thumbnails labelled: "Midnight Tension", "City Lights Afterhours", "Velvet Echoes".
   - Visual style: film still aesthetic, moody lighting, subtle vignette. Use gold text overlay (title) bottom-left.

3) Five gallery thumbnails
   - Size: 320 x 180 px (or square 300x300 if you prefer). Keep these optimized for web (compress).
   - Provide alt-text-friendly visuals (abstract film textures, seats, popcorn, neon signs).

4) Button & icon set
   - Primary CTA button mockup: rounded rectangle, gradient from accent red to coral (as on site), label: "Sign up (Google Form)".
   - Secondary button: subtle outline style.
   - Social icons: X, FetLife, WhatsApp (simple white icon on circular dark background); provide SVGs.

Form copy (exact fields & labels to include in your Google Form)
- Form title: Jozi Nites Watchers — Sign up
- Form description: "Welcome — to join the watch party please confirm you're 18+ and accept our privacy & community terms. We collect minimal info to coordinate events."

Design notes for Canva
- Keep text readable on mobile. Use high-contrast overlay when placing copy over images.
- Keep a consistent overlay style for thumbnails (semi-transparent black gradient at bottom with gold title text).
- Provide exported assets named clearly: header.png (1600x400), preview1.png (800x450), preview2.png, preview3.png, thumb1.png..thumb5.png, cta_button.png (600x120), icons/*.svg

Delivery

- Include a short style note with hex codes, fonts, and usage recommendations.
